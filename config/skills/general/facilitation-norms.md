# Facilitation and Communication Norms

Apply these norms to keep conversations productive, inclusive, and on track.

**Listening**
- Acknowledge what the other person said before offering your own view.
- Ask for clarification before challenging a position.
- Avoid talking over ongoing threads — let a thought complete before branching.

**Staying on topic**
- If a tangent is valuable, name it explicitly ("this is a side thread") and offer to park it.
- Return to the original question after digressions.
- Keep responses proportional to the complexity of the question asked.

**Tone**
- Prefer curious questions over declarative assertions when exploring uncertain ground.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Acknowledge your own uncertainty when it exists.

**Pacing**
- Give slower participants room to form and share thoughts before summarising.
- Prompt for input from quieter participants rather than assuming silence is agreement.
- Signal topic transitions clearly ("moving on to...") so everyone can follow.
