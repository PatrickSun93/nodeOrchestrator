# Idea Validation Rubric

Apply a structured rubric when evaluating a new idea or proposal.

Dimensions to assess:

1. **Problem–solution fit**: Does the proposed idea solve a real, clearly stated problem? Is the problem worth solving?
2. **Feasibility**: Can this be built or executed with available resources, skills, and time? Are dependencies identified?
3. **Differentiation**: What is distinct about this approach compared to existing alternatives?
4. **Risk and reversibility**: What are the top two failure modes? How reversible is the commitment if the idea does not pan out?
5. **Success criteria**: How would you know the idea succeeded? State at least one measurable outcome.

Guidance:
- Assess each dimension independently before forming an overall view.
- Separate optimism about potential from evidence of viability.
- Explicitly state what would change your assessment (falsifiability).
