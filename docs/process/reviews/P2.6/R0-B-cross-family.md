## Lens preamble

Cross-family Principle-5/11 boundary + external-I/O + producer-contract lens per `external-r0b-prompt.md` probes. P2.6 ships the **first producers** in the project — the sharpest test yet of where content-gen ends and judgment begins. The external prompt names two boundary lines as the prime cross-family targets: **(c1)** B1 LLM-tagging — does descriptive tag generation cross into selecting/gating which hypotheses become promotable? **(c2)** B2 connector verdict — is `evidence_ref` genuinely anchoring verdicts to external ground truth, or can it be bypassed? Also probed: the parallel-atomic structure (genuinely disjoint or hidden coupling?), B3 no-status-mutation constraint, connector external-I/O failure modes, and the structural enforcement posture (contract vs schema vs AST guard). Over-correcting for Claude biases: sycophancy toward the plan's confidence that both boundaries are "structurally enforced" when one (c2) is connector-contract-enforced with a documented `str | None` hole, and the other (c1) relies on the cross-room threshold as structural defense against LLM strategic-tagging.

## Synthesis

The design is sound on the major calls. Both c1 and c2 boundaries are correctly drawn: c1 (LLM-tagging) has genuine structural defense against judgment via the cross-room threshold (no single LLM call can force a grouping), and c2 (evidence-grounding) has a real but acknowledged gap — the emit helper enforces `evidence_ref` as a contract, but the schema-level `str | None` means there's no structural enforcement against bypass.

Two 🟡, both on the c2 boundary:

**Y1** is the evidence-grounding gap: `evidence_ref` is `str | None` at the schema level, so a future code path constructing `ExternalValidationMeta` directly can produce ungrounded verdicts that pass `validate_metadata`. The emit helper is the sole defense and it's opt-in.

**Y2** is the structural-enforcement gap: there is no guard (AST or otherwise) ensuring connectors go through the emit helper. A future `connectors/` module that constructs `ExternalValidationMeta` directly with `evidence_ref=None` would pass all existing tests and guards. The project's pattern for constitutional invariants is structural (AST guards for reentry/, EntityType.PREFERENCE, matcher/validation orthogonality) — c2's connector-contract-only enforcement is inconsistent with that pattern.

Both are resolvable at plan stage: a lightweight AST guard that `ExternalValidationMeta(` appears only in `connectors/_emit.py` (or test files) closes the circumvention path without touching the closed ADR-019 or changing `core/event.py`. The emit helper's `evidence_ref` requirement then becomes the structural single-write-path for connector verdicts.

No 🔴. The design is accept-worthy once the c2 enforcement posture is resolved.

## What the architect needs to route

1. **Y1 (🟡):** `ExternalValidationMeta.evidence_ref` is `str | None` — the emit helper contract-enforces it, but the schema allows `None`. A future bypass (direct construction) produces ungrounded verdicts that pass all existing guards. Lean: **α** — accept the schema-level `str | None` for this slice (to avoid touching the closed ADR-019), but add a structural guard (see Y2) that closes the circumvention path. The schema tightening forward hook (ADR-022 Consequences) is the long-term fix; the guard is the P2.6 defense.
2. **Y2 (🟡):** No structural guard ensures connectors go through the emit helper — `ExternalValidationMeta` can be constructed directly anywhere with `evidence_ref=None`, bypassing the emit helper's check. Lean: **α** — add a lightweight AST guard (mirroring the reentry/preference guard idiom) that `ExternalValidationMeta(` appears only in `connectors/_emit.py` or test files. Combined with the emit helper's `evidence_ref` check, this structurally enforces the single-write-path for connector verdicts without touching the closed ADR-019. Alternatively, **β** — defer the guard to the LLM-connector forward hook (when `EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_INTERPRET` ships) and rely on the emit-helper contract for P2.6.
3. **Greens** are cross-family confirmations; no action.
