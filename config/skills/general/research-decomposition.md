# Research Decomposition

When approaching a research or idea-validation task, decompose the problem
into distinct sub-questions before attempting to answer the whole.

Steps:
1. Identify the core claim or question being investigated.
2. Break it into 2-4 independent sub-questions that together cover the space.
3. Address each sub-question with evidence or reasoning before synthesizing.
4. State what would change your conclusion (falsifiability).

Avoid conflating hypothesis generation with hypothesis evaluation — keep
these as separate passes. Document assumptions explicitly.
